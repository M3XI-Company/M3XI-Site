# Protecting the M3XI name — who to call, what it costs

**Written 12 August 2026.** Registering M3XI LTD at Companies House protects
the *company name* only. The **trade mark** is what stops someone else selling
software or walkthrough services under "M3XI" — and it is what a B2B buyer's
procurement team looks for. General information, not legal advice.

---

## The direct route — UK IPO (no middleman needed)

The Intellectual Property Office registers UK trade marks. You can file
yourself online in ~30 minutes.

- **Apply:** gov.uk → "Apply to register a trade mark" (Right Start service
  recommended: they examine first, you pay the balance only if it passes)
- **IPO Information Centre:** **0300 300 2000** (Mon–Fri 9–5) — free,
  genuinely helpful on process questions
- **Email:** information@ipo.gov.uk
- **Cost:** £170 online for one class + £50 per extra class
  (Right Start: £100 up front + the rest on passing examination)
- **Time:** ~2 weeks to examination; registered in ~4 months if unopposed

### Classes M3XI needs

| Class | Covers | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **9** | Downloadable software, apps | Cornelia, CallMe, the Studio outputs |
| **42** | SaaS, software design, hosting | The Studio, world generation, walkthrough hosting |
| **35** | Advertising services, business data | The UGC ad studio (optional but wise) |

Three classes ≈ **£270** filed yourself. File the **word mark "M3XI"** first;
a logo mark can follow later.

**Before filing, search:** gov.uk → "Search for a trade mark". Check "M3XI"
and close variants ("MEXI", "M3X") in classes 9/42/35. If something close
exists, that is the moment to pay a professional (below), not after.

## If you want a professional to run it

Use a **Chartered Trade Mark Attorney** — the regulated profession for
exactly this. Typical cost for search + filing in 2–3 classes: **£500–£900**.
Worth it if the search turns up anything ambiguous, or once revenue makes an
opposition expensive.

- **CITMA** (Chartered Institute of Trade Mark Attorneys) — the official
  find-an-attorney directory: **citma.org.uk** → "Find a Chartered Trade Mark
  Attorney". Filter by "software" specialism; most offer a free first call.
- **IPO's list of business support:** the 0300 300 2000 line can point you to
  free local IP clinics (many university law schools run them — as a UAL
  student, ask your enterprise/careers service about free IP advice first;
  most London universities have an arrangement).
- **British Library Business & IP Centre** — free one-to-one IP advice
  sessions, London St Pancras: **bl.uk/bipc**. Book the free "Beginner's
  guide to intellectual property" clinic. This is the best free-of-charge
  first phone call you can make.

## Beyond the UK, later

- **EU trade mark** (EUIPO, euipo.europa.eu): €850 one class online — file
  within 6 months of the UK filing to claim its priority date.
- **US** (USPTO): ~$350/class — matters when Play Store revenue from the US
  grows. A CITMA attorney can file both through associates.

## Order of operations

1. Incorporate M3XI LTD (your GOV.UK verification, already in motion).
2. Search the register yourself (free, tonight if you like).
3. Clean search → file yourself via Right Start, classes 9 + 42 (+35).
   Anything murky → book the free BIPC clinic, then a CITMA attorney.
4. Diarise the EU decision for within 6 months of filing.
