Account & data

Delete your account.

You can delete your M3XI account and its data at any time — from inside the app, or by asking us here if you no longer have it installed. No account is required to make the request.

Last updated: 6 August 2026 · Contact: support@m3xi.com

CallMe

Deleting your CallMe account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open CallMe and go to Settings (the gear, top right of the home screen).
  2. Scroll to Delete account & data.
  3. Confirm. Your account is closed immediately.

Without the app

Email support@m3xi.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my CallMe account”. Sending it from your registered address is how we confirm it is really you — if you cannot, tell us and we will verify another way. We action every request within 30 days and confirm by email when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your profile — name, age, city, bio, gender, orientation, ethnicity, interests, occupation and education
  • Your MeCards, including every photo you uploaded
  • Your matching preferences
  • Your match and call history, and your connections
  • Your identity verification record
  • Your wallet and subscription record held by us

What we keep, and why

  • Safety records — bans and reports made about your account. These are kept so that a removed account cannot simply be recreated, and so we can still act on a report made by someone else. They are held for up to 24 months.
  • Records we are legally required to retain, such as purchase records needed for tax and accounting.

Everything else is erased or irreversibly anonymised within 30 days.

Deleting some of your data, without closing your account

You do not have to delete everything to delete something. In the app you can, at any time and without losing your account:

  • remove an individual photo from a MeCard, or delete a whole MeCard;
  • clear your bio, occupation, education, orientation or ethnicity — every one of those fields is optional and can be emptied;
  • clear your ethnicity matching preference, which stops it being used immediately;
  • turn off location, which stops distance being used to match you.

For anything you cannot remove yourself — a specific photo you have lost access to, your identity verification record, or your call history — email support@m3xi.com and say what you want removed. We will do it and confirm, and your account stays open.

Worth knowing

  • Your subscription is separate. Deleting your account does not cancel Premium — that is billed by Apple or Google. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account, or it will keep renewing.
  • Unused coins go with the account. Coins cannot be refunded or moved to another account, so spend them first if you want to.
  • Your ID documents were never ours to delete. Verification is carried out by Didit; document images and selfies go to them and never reach our servers. We only ever held the outcome, and that is deleted with your account; the safety records above keep only a reference to the verification session, never the result or any document.

M3XI Spatial

Deleting an M3XI Spatial account

Walkthroughs and tours. Unpublish or delete any walkthrough or tour yourself, at any time, from your M3XI Spatial console at m3xi.com/console. Deleting a tour removes its photographs and scans from our storage.

Your account. Email support@m3xi.com from the address you sign in with. We delete your account, profile, walkthroughs and uploads. Enquiries your walkthroughs received are deleted too unless you ask us to keep them for you; we keep billing records where the law requires it.

If you sent an enquiry to an agency. Contact the agency, or email us with the property and roughly when you sent it and we will remove it.

Cornelia and AutoUV are on hold. If you joined a waitlist, tested Cornelia, or hold an AutoUV licence, email support@m3xi.com and we will delete what we hold. Waitlists: use the unsubscribe link in any email we have sent you, or email us.

Your rights

Other data requests

As well as deletion, you can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct anything inaccurate, or to restrict or object to particular processing. Email support@m3xi.com and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle a request, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or to your local supervisory authority in the EEA.

See also our privacy policy and terms.
