Open in CallMe

Crews hang out in the CallMe app. Open your friend’s link on your phone to join them.

Crews are for friends

Only friends of the crew’s host can join with it, and it stops working when the crew closes. New to CallMe? Ask your friend for their invite link as well: join with it and you’ll find each other in Friends. Then tap this link again.

Where a crew can go

The crew’s host picks one of three, and the crew goes once everyone’s ready.

  • Just usYour crew’s own private table, nobody else.
  • Sit at a tablePull up chairs at an open table, together.
  • Find another crewHang out with another crew who’s looking too.

Nobody goes anywhere until they tap Ready themselves.
