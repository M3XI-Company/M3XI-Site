CallMe card designs

A design copies my work.

If a card design on CallMe uses your artwork, your characters or your logo without your permission, tell us. A person on our team reads every report, and when a design is taken down, every copy of it goes too.

safety@m3xi.com

Please include:

  • what the design looks like, its title if you saw it, and a screenshot of the card if you have one;
  • your original work, or a link to where it was first published, and roughly when you made it;
  • your name, how to reach you, and whether the work is yours or you are acting for the person or company it belongs to;
  • a line saying that what you have sent is true, as far as you know.

On CallMe and offered a copy of it? You can also report it from the gift letter: choose It copies somebody’s work. If a design of yours was taken down and you believe it is your own work, email the same address with what shows it is yours, and a person will look again.
