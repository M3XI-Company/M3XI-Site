How it works

One coach, four things it does for you.

  1. Your AI coach

    Chat with a coach that knows your goal. Ask it for a meal plan or a workout and it builds one for you, then adjusts as you go.

  2. Progress you can see

    Every workout is saved to your history, and your goals show how far you have come and what is left.

  3. Gym buddies near you

    Breeze matches you with people nearby who train like you do, so you have someone to turn up for.

  4. Meals from our partners

    Short on time? Breeze works with quick-meal businesses, so a meal that fits your plan is easy to order.
