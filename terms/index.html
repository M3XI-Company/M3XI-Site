Automated safety tools. These tools can warn you during a call, remove you from it, and give a strike, suspend or permanently ban an account without a person being involved first; a person reviews every permanent ban afterwards. A walkthrough built from photographs, a floor plan or a description is AI-generated. It carries an AI-generated label, which you must not remove, hide or crop out. You are responsible for making sure your listings comply with the law that applies to them, including consumer protection law and the rules on what information must be given to buyers and tenants.
