How it works

Three steps to a real conversation.

No profile to write, no opening line to agonise over. Set yourself up once, then let CallMe find someone.

Your first call

  1. Check your age, once.Everyone on CallMe is 18 or over and has passed an ID check. You do it one time, in the app, and it is done.
  2. Press the letter three times.CallMe finds someone who is online now. The only setting is how far to look: My country or International.
  3. Talk.Their cards are dealt first, so you can see who you are meeting. The call starts as voice, and cameras come on only if you both ask.

Go on, say hello.

Finding someone who is online now…

On every call

  • Skip after the first minute, and we find you someone new.
  • Ask to be friends if it went well, and chat whenever you like.
  • Sit at a table with a few people around a topic, if one to one is not your thing.
  • Report or block from any call. A report never says who made it.

Meet the dog.

A small cartoon dog now lives in CallMe, drawn from our founder’s own. It shows you round on your first day, keeps you up to date with your friends, and it is always ready for a walk.

  • Your first day

    A guided tour

    It walks you through calling, your card and poster, friends, the map and walks. Take the tour again any time from Settings.

  • Tap it

    News from friends

    Hear who is at a table, who has posted a snapshot and who is off to an event. Only friends, and only those who choose to share.

  • Hold, then let go

    The friends map

    Hold the dog still until it points, then let go, and the map of your friends opens.

  • Press and shake

    A walk

    Keep a finger on the dog and give your phone a shake to set off. Do the same again to stop.

Rather not have a dog about? You can hide it in Settings, under Your dog.

Make your own

Your own cards, your own wall.

A MeCard is what people see when they meet you on CallMe. A poster is what friends see when they open you, and people you’ve met too if you let them. Both are yours to make, and a card you design can be given to the people you talk to.

  • In the app

    Put up a poster

    Go to Settings, then My poster. Choose a paper, then pin, tape and twist your photos and words into place. It is drawn on your phone, so you see it exactly as your visitors will.

  • In the app

    Make your cards

    From your poster, choose Edit my cards. Pick a paper, add a photo, stickers and your words, and the cards people see when you match are yours.

  • On a computer

    Design card art of your own

    Bring a picture you made yourself, or build one from the papers and stickers, and sign in with your phone. A person reads every design. Then it lands in your collection, and you can give up to twenty copies to people you have met on a call.

All questions How we keep calls safe
What is CallMe?

CallMe is for meeting people by talking to them. Press the letter three times and you are in a voice call with someone who is online now, instead of swiping through profiles and waiting for replies.

It is a place to meet people and have a conversation. Dating is welcome if that is where it goes, but it is not the point.

Does my camera come on straight away?

No. Every one-to-one call starts as voice. Cameras come on only when both of you have asked. One person can never switch on the other person’s camera, and you can turn yours off at any time without ending the call. Tables have their own camera rule, set by the host.

Who can use CallMe?

Adults aged 18 and over. Everyone verifies their age before they can call anyone, with an identity document, a live selfie and a face match. The check is carried out by our verification provider, and we receive the result rather than your documents.

Can I choose who I am matched with?

There is one setting: My country or International. It goes by the flag you pick on your profile, not by where your phone is. Beyond being 18 or over, nobody is filtered by age, gender or distance. Anyone you block is not matched with you again while the block stands.

Does CallMe know where I am?

Only while CallMe is open, and only for things you start. A walk uses your location to measure the distance. The friends map is off until you turn it on; then only the friends you tick can see you, by town or precisely, and you can pause it for a day. CallMe never asks for your location in the background.

How much does CallMe cost?

CallMe is free to use, with five calls a day. You can earn more by watching an advert or by completing weekly achievements. CallMe Premium removes the daily limit and gives you a head start in matching, tables for up to twenty people and the creator studio. Premium and call credits are optional, and you’ll find them, with the price where you live, inside the app.
