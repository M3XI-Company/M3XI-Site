How it works

Three steps to a real conversation.

No profile to write, no opening line to agonise over. Set yourself up once, then let the app find someone.

  1. Verify once

    Everyone on CallMe is an adult who has passed an age check. You do it one time, in the app, and it is done.

  2. Get matched both ways

    Say who you would like to meet. A match only happens when your preferences suit each other, so it is never one-sided.

  3. Talk

    The call opens on voice. Cameras come on when you both ask. Not clicking? Skip, and you are both somewhere new in seconds.

Make your own

Cards you design, given by hand.

A MeCard is what somebody sees when they meet you. You can design one of your own — on a computer, at your own pace — and give copies of it to the people you have actually talked to.

  1. Design it on a computer

    Bring a picture you made yourself, or build one out of the four papers and the stickers. You set where the wearer's own face sits on your card. Sign in by scanning a code with your phone — there is no password.

  2. It lands in your collection

    Every design is read by a person before anyone can be given one. When it passes, it appears in your collection in the app, in with the cards you have earned.

  3. Give a copy to someone

    Twenty copies of a design, ever. You give them from your phone, to people you have met on a call — and the one who keeps it wears it on a card of their own.

What is CallMe?

CallMe is a video call app for meeting new people. Instead of swiping through profiles and waiting for replies, you get matched with someone and you are talking to them straight away.

Does my camera come on straight away?

No. Every call opens on voice. Video turns on only when both of you ask for it. One person can never switch on the other person's camera, and you can turn yours off at any time without ending the call.

Who can use CallMe?

Adults aged 18 and over. Everyone verifies their age before they can call anyone, with an identity document, a live selfie and a face match. The check is carried out by our verification provider, and we receive the result rather than your documents.

How much does CallMe cost?

CallMe is free to use, with five calls a day. You can earn more by watching an advert or by completing weekly achievements. CallMe Premium removes the daily limit and gives you priority matching and extra card styles, for £8.49 a month or £71.99 a year in the UK; other countries are priced by the store. Coins are optional, sold in packs from £4.99, and spent on extras in the app.
