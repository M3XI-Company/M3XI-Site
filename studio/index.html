Walkthroughs that sell the viewing.

Built in-house

the part nobody else sells

A walkthrough is only as good as its geometry. Our own proprietary pipeline works out what a scanned space actually is before a buyer ever opens it, so rooms stand the right way up, walls stop you, and the parts the camera missed are admitted rather than papered over.

measured, not guessed

Measured once

When a scan is attached we measure it: which way is up, where the floor is, where every wall stands and how much the camera never reached. The answers are stored with the walkthrough, so every buyer walks the same room.

the right way up

Reads the room

We find up by looking for what makes a room a room: a floor, clear headroom above it, a ceiling over that, and things that rest on the floor rather than hang in the air. For an unusual capture, one click in the viewer fixes it for everyone.

solid walls

A floor you can walk

A map of the floor, sixteen centimetres to a square, decides where a person can stand. Walls block you, furniture you could not walk through stops you, and nothing taller than a stair can be climbed.

walk in, look along

An entrance that faces the room

A walkthrough opens where a person would walk in, looking along the longest clear view that ends at a wall — not at the carpet, and not out into a gap in the scan.

plan in, house out

A house from its floor plan

Upload the plan with photographs of each room. The plan is read into rooms and which of them connect, each room is built as its own walkable space, the rooms are joined by doors, and the street outside comes from the postcode.

holes stay holes

Gaps are reported, never filled

Where the camera never looked, we say how much is missing and whether to scan again. Nothing in a scanned walkthrough is invented, and the viewer says so in the frame.

Two kinds, one label

a listing can never pass one off as the other
Scanned

A record of the property

  • Captured on a phone with Scaniverse, or from 360° photographs taken on one visit.
  • What a buyer sees is what the camera recorded, at true scale in metres.
  • The right choice for any property that is actually on the market.
AI-generated

A visualisation, and it says so

  • Built from photographs of a room, a floor plan, or a written description.
  • For plots and new-builds that do not exist yet, refurbishment ideas and staging.
  • Carries an “AI-generated” mark wherever it appears. Never presented as the property itself.

How an agency uses it

from the viewing to the listing
01

Capture or build

Walk the property with a phone, take 360° photos at /capture/, or give the Spatial Engine a floor plan and photographs.

02

Check it privately

A preview link for you and the vendor. Set the entrance, place notes on the fixtures that matter. Nothing is public until you press Go live.

03

Share and take enquiries

One link or one embed on the listing. Every enquiry arrives in your dashboard with the buyer’s details and the room they were standing in.
