World Engine

a floor plan you can walk through

Two kinds of world, and each one says which it is. Scanned worlds come from a real place captured on a phone — the capture is the truth, nothing is invented. Generated worlds are AI-made from a prompt or reference photos, labelled “AI-generated” wherever they appear, and never passed off as a real space. Either kind publishes as one link or a tag on your own site.

01

Scan

Walk the space with Scaniverse (free, iPhone & Android) in Splat mode. Five minutes, true scale in metres.

02

Publish

Drop the file into the World Viewer. Set the view, place info points and 3D assets, brand it, press Publish.

03

Walk

Visitors open one link on any device and walk the real space — every enquiry is kept in your dashboard.
