Spatial Engine

a property you can walk through

Scan a place on a phone, or build one from a floor plan. Either way it becomes a space a buyer can walk in a browser, from one link, with no app and no headset. Scanned places come from a real capture and nothing in them is invented. Generated places are AI-made and labelled as such everywhere they appear, so a listing can never pass one off as a photograph.

01

Scan

Walk the property with Scaniverse in Splat mode — free, on an iPhone or an Android. About five minutes a room, at true scale in metres.

02

Publish

Drop the file into the viewer. It is measured once — which way is up, where the floor is, where the walls are, how much the camera never saw — then you set the entrance, place the notes and press Publish.

03

Share

One link on the listing, the window card or a WhatsApp message. Every enquiry from inside the tour lands in your dashboard.

For estate agents

The viewing before the viewing

  • Every listing walkable — a buyer moves through the rooms instead of swiping eleven photographs.
  • Captured on the phone you own — no £5,000 camera, no crew, no closing early.
  • Measured, not guessed — floor area, ceiling height and the parts the camera never reached are reported rather than filled in.
  • Fewer wasted viewings — the people who book have already walked it.
  • Yours to embed — one link or one tag on your own site, and the enquiry button sits inside the tour.

Hosting, branding, lead capture and embeds come with the business plan — see the pricing. Scanning is quoted per site.

Build a place

when there is nothing to scan yet

A room from photographs of that room, a whole house from its floor plan, or somewhere that does not exist at all. Everything built here is AI-generated and carries that label — it is for concepts, staging ideas and listings that have not been built yet, never for showing a property that exists.

Prompt page

Describe the place

Up to 8 photos of one room, taken from nearby spots so they overlap. For a whole house, open the viewer and add the floor plan — it builds every room and joins them by doors.

Finished place 150 credits, draft 40. A 3D object is 15 credits, built from your first attachment or your last generated image. Finished places publish to the Library with an AI-generated badge. Packs on Pricing.

Advanced — which model builds it

Draft first is the cheap way to see whether an idea holds up; generate the finished version once it does. Places render through M3XI Cloud — there is no key to paste and nothing is billed to you beyond the credits shown.
