Property tours for estate agents

A tour is built from real 360° photographs of the property — one per standpoint, taken on one short visit with a 360 camera. A buyer opens a link on any phone or laptop, looks around freely from each standpoint, and walks between them on the floorplan. Nothing is invented: no AI imagery is ever part of a property tour.

  • Preview before it goes live — a private link for you and the vendor; nothing is public until you press Go live.
  • Enquiries in your dashboard — every one is kept, with the buyer’s name, how to reach them, and the room they were standing in.
  • Embed on the listing — one tag on your own page, or share the link and QR.
  • Take it down any time — unpublish or delete from the dashboard.

How it runs

  • 1 — Capture on your phone at /capture/: stand in each spot, take the 360 photo, pin it on the floorplan, upload.
  • 2 — Check it in the dashboard: preview, then Go live when it is right.
  • 3 — Share the link or the embed; read enquiries in the same dashboard; take the tour down whenever the property is gone.
