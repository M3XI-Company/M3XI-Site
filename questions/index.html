The basics

What is CallMe?

CallMe is a video call app for meeting new people. Instead of swiping through profiles and waiting for replies, you get matched with someone and you are talking to them straight away.

What does “ditch the card games” mean?

Most apps for meeting people are a card game: swipe through profiles, collect matches, then wait on a chat that dies after two messages. CallMe skips all of that. The first thing you do with someone new is talk to them.

Is CallMe a dating app?

No. CallMe is for meeting people and having a conversation. Dating is welcome if that is where a conversation goes, but it is not the point, and nobody on CallMe is a profile to be sorted through.

Who makes CallMe?

M3XI LTD, a small company in London. It was founded by Michael and co-founded with Leo Chi. Meet the team.

Calls and matching

How does matching work?

You choose your reach: people in your own country, or people in other countries. CallMe then introduces you to someone who is free to talk right now. Everyone you meet has passed the same age check, and anyone you block is never matched with you again. You can change your reach whenever you like.

Does my camera come on straight away?

No. Every call opens on voice. Video turns on only when both of you ask for it. One person can never switch on the other person's camera, and you can turn yours off at any time without ending the call.

What if the conversation isn't going anywhere?

Skip. You are both on your way to someone new in seconds, and nobody has to explain themselves. If it was a good conversation, you can ask to be friends instead.

Can I talk to more than one person?

Yes. Open a table around a topic and a few people can sit down together, or pull up a chair at someone else's. Whoever is in your party comes with you.

Are there things to do on a call?

Nine games you can start mid-call for everyone on it at once: two truths and a lie, charades, categories, would you rather, 21 questions, sketch together, memory, a crossword and a quick quiz.

Making cards

How do I make a card?

In the app, go to Settings, then My poster, then Edit my cards. Pick a paper, add stickers and your words, and the card is yours.

CallMe Premium members can also design card art of their own on a computer, at m3xi.com/design: bring a picture you made yourself, or build one from the papers and stickers. Every design is read by a person before anyone can be given one. Then it lands in your collection in the app, and you can give copies to people you have met on a call.

Who can use the creator studio?

It comes with CallMe Premium, in the app. You also need to have passed the age check and agreed to the creator terms, which are in the app under Settings, then Creator terms.

After that, open m3xi.com/design on a computer and scan the code on the screen with your phone. There is no password.

What happens to a card I was given if the creator leaves?

It stays yours. Your copy stays on your cards and in your collection if the person who made it retires the design, stops making cards or deletes their account. If they have left CallMe, the copy says so and shows nothing more about them.

A copy only goes if our team takes the design down, for example because it copied someone else's work. If that happens you get a letter saying so, and nothing else about your account changes. A copy is yours to wear. It cannot be passed on or sold.

If you think a design copies your own work, tell us here.

Safety and privacy

Who can use CallMe?

Adults aged 18 and over. Everyone verifies their age before they can call anyone, with an identity document, a live selfie and a face match. The check is carried out by our verification provider, and we receive the result rather than your documents.

How do I stay safe on a call?

On every call you can silence someone for yourself, block them, or report them. A report never tells the other person who filed it. A block works both ways, so neither of you can be matched with the other, and you can undo it in Settings. Verification proves someone is a real adult. It is not a background check, so never share financial details or your home address.

Our child safety standards explain how we keep under-18s out.

Are calls recorded?

No. CallMe does not record the audio or video of your calls.

What we do collect, and why, is set out in the CallMe privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings, then Delete account and data. If you no longer have the app, email support@m3xi.com from the address you signed up with; m3xi.com/delete-account explains how.

Cost and devices

How much does CallMe cost?

CallMe is free to use, with five calls a day. You can earn more by watching an advert or by completing weekly achievements. CallMe Premium removes the daily limit and gives you a head start in matching, tables for up to twenty people and the creator studio. Premium and call credits are optional, and you'll find them, with the price where you live, inside the app.
