ARGOS retells the moment Odysseus comes home after twenty years and is recognised only by his dog. The dog is a real robot, and I drove it live on stage opposite actor Edmund Digby-Jones.

The piece is about trust, error and intimacy between people and machines, so every movement had to read as feeling to the audience, not as mechanics.
