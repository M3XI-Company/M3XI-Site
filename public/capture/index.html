M3XI CAPTURE

A TOUR FROM 360 PHOTOS

A standpoint is a spot a buyer can stand on and look around: one full 360 photo per standpoint, straight from the 360 camera's app (a phone photosphere app will do at a pinch). Photos must be complete 360 images — twice as wide as they are tall.

How to shoot
  1. Stand in the middle of the room, camera at about 1.5 m (chest height) on a stick or tripod.
  2. One photo per standpoint. Standpoints 2–3 m apart; one in each doorway so the rooms join up.
  3. Daylight on, lights on. Step out of the shot or stand directly under the camera.
  4. Export the stitched 360 image from the camera's app, then add it here.

THE FLOORPLAN

Add the floorplan off the listing. Then tap a standpoint, and tap where it is on the plan. Buyers get the plan beside the tour, and the arrows between standpoints come from these pins.

WHICH WAY IS WHICH

For each pinned standpoint, turn the photo until you are facing a neighbouring standpoint and tap “That way”. That lines the photo up with the plan so the arrows point the right way.
