Image

your GPU does the work

Bonsai Image runs entirely on your own graphics card through WebGPU. It needs a desktop browser with WebGPU (Chrome or Edge) and downloads about 1.2 GB once, then works offline-fast. Phones will not run it. If you want images made on our cloud instead — bigger models, no download, saved to your account — that is the paid Generation bench.

How this is free

no catch

Your browser does the work

The models are downloaded to your machine and run there, on your CPU or GPU. Your text and images never leave your computer.

We pay nothing per generation

No server of ours is involved. There is no cost to M3XI when you press Generate, so there is nothing to charge you for.

That is why it is unlimited

Make as much as you like. The paid Studio exists for bigger models, video, 3D and a Library that keeps your work — it runs on our cloud and costs credits.
