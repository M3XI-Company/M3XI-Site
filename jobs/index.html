Jobs at M3XI

Help us build the studio.

We’re a small group building Cornelia, AutoUV and CallMe — and aiming at robotics beyond that. We’d love more curious hands. Read the honest bit first, then pick a role.

The honest bit — read before applying

  • We are not funded and not yet a registered company. M3XI is in the middle of being set up.
  • These are unpaid, volunteer co-builder roles for now — there is no salary yet. If and when M3XI is registered and revenue arrives, the people who built it with us are first in line.
  • What we can offer today: real shipped products for your portfolio, honest credit for everything you make, a small kind team, and a genuine say in what M3XI becomes.
  • Everything is remote and flexible around your studies or job.

If that excites you rather than puts you off — you’re exactly who we’re looking for.

Open roles · apply via support@m3xi.com
