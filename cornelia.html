What Cornelia does

From lecture to exam-ready.

Record & transcribe

Tap record in class. Cornelia captures the lecture and turns speech into clean, searchable text.

Cornell, outline & mind-map

Your transcript becomes structured notes — cue columns, summaries and layouts made for revision, not just storage.

Photo-to-notes

Point your camera at a whiteboard or a handwritten page and Cornelia lifts it into tidy digital notes.

Organised by subject

Every course and topic in its own place. Nothing gets lost between September and exam season.

Built for revision

Summaries, key terms and checklists come out of your own notes — so revising feels like a head start.

Calm by design

No clutter, no noise. Open the app, take the note, get back to listening.
