How it works

See them. Keep them. Talk again.

Set yourself up once (an ID check, your card and, if you like, your socials), then say hello.

From a call to keeping them

  1. See who you’re talking to.Tear the letter on your home screen and choose Someone new, or sit at a table with a few people. You see their name, flag and card before the call. Video starts when you both have it on, and you see yourself first.
  2. Keep in touch.Get on? After a couple of minutes you can both tap Keep in touch. If you both do, you see each other’s socials. A tap that isn’t returned is never shown to anyone.
  3. Start a group chat.After a table, start a group chat with everyone you sat with: anyone you’re not yet in touch with gets an invite and chooses. Then message and call each other any time, for free.

Go on, say hello.

Sending…

On every call

  • Leave whenever you like. Every call ends on a summary, never in another call, and nobody has to explain themselves.
  • Keep in touch if you click: their socials, a group chat, and calls for free.
  • Face to Face is a table where cameras are on, so you see everyone who’s there.
  • Report or block from any call. A report never says who made it.
Hello

The dog

Meet the dog.

A small cartoon dog now lives in CallMe, drawn from our founder’s own. It shows you round on your first day, keeps you up to date with your friends, and it is always ready for a walk.

  • Your first day

    A guided tour

    It shows you how to video call, keep in touch and find the people you talked to, then, if you like, the map and walks. Take the tour again any time from About CallMe, in Settings.

  • Tap it

    News from friends

    Hear who is at a table, who has posted a snapshot and who is off to an event. Only friends, and only those who choose to share.

  • Tap it twice

    The friends map

    Tap the dog twice, quickly, and the map of your friends opens.

  • Hold, then let go

    A walk

    Hold the dog still until it starts to run, then let go to set off. Or keep a finger on it and give your phone a shake.

Rather not have a dog about? You can hide it in Settings, under Your dog.

Make your own

Your own cards, your own wall.

A MeCard is what people see before your camera comes on. A poster is what friends see when they open you, and people you’ve met too if you let them. Both are yours to make, and a card you design can be given to the people you talk to.

  • In the app

    Put up a poster

    Go to Settings, then My poster. Choose a paper, then pin, tape and twist your photos and words into place. It is drawn on your phone, so you see it exactly as your visitors will.

  • In the app

    Make your cards

    From your poster, choose Edit my cards. Pick a paper, add a photo, stickers and your words, and the cards people see when you talk are yours.

  • On a computer

    Design card art of your own

    Bring a picture you made yourself, or build one from the papers and stickers, and sign in with your phone. A person reads every design. Then it lands in your collection, and you can give up to twenty copies to people you have met on a call.

All questions How we keep calls safe
What is CallMe?

CallMe is a video calling app for adults. See who you’re talking to, one-to-one or at a table with a few people. If you click, you both tap Keep in touch: you see each other’s socials, you can start a group chat, and you can talk again for free.

Everyone is 18 or over and has passed an ID check. Nobody is anonymous, nobody on CallMe is a profile to be sorted through, and nothing in it is built for dating.

Is it a video call app?

Yes. A one-to-one call opens on video when you both choose it before the call, and either of you can stay on voice. You see yourself first, and a new person’s video stays blurred until our server has checked it. One person can never switch on the other person’s camera, and you can turn yours off at any time without ending the call. At tables your camera is yours: one tap turns it on, and Face to Face is the table where cameras are on.

Who sees my socials?

Only people you keep in touch with: you both tapped Keep in touch after a call or a table. A tap that isn’t returned is never shown to anyone. You can hide any of your socials from any one person, and if you stop keeping in touch, your socials and your messages with them close at once. Once someone has followed you on another app, CallMe can’t undo that, so block them there too if you need to.

Who can use CallMe?

Adults aged 18 and over. Everyone verifies their age before they can call anyone, with an identity document, a live selfie and a face match. The check is carried out by our verification provider, and we receive the result rather than your documents. For the photo check, your verification selfie passes through our server's memory to compare it with your card photo. CallMe never stores your ID images or selfie.

Who will I talk to one-to-one?

You choose how far to look: My country or International. It goes by the flag you pick on your profile, not by where your phone is. Within that, CallMe leans towards people you are likely to get on with, from what people are into and which calls go on for a while, and there is nothing to fill in. Beyond being 18 or over, nobody is filtered by age, gender or distance, and anyone you block is never put with you again while the block stands. You can switch the personalising off in Settings, under Legal and privacy, Personalised pairing.

Does CallMe know where I am?

Only while CallMe is open, and only for things you start. A walk uses your location to measure the distance. Sharing your place on the friends map is off until you turn it on; then only the friends you tick can see you, by town or precisely, and it switches itself off 24 hours later. CallMe never asks for your location in the background.

How much does CallMe cost?

CallMe is free to use. Video, keeping in touch, messages, group chats and calls with friends are free, always. One-to-ones and opening a table use your five free calls a day, and you can earn more by watching an advert or from This week at CallMe, a few ideas each week that each add a call or more. CallMe Premium is for hosts: tables for up to twenty people, opening any table at no cost, camera looks and the creator studio, with a big allowance of calls on top. Premium and call credits are optional, and you’ll find them, with the price where you live, inside the app.
